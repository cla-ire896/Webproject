BACKGROUND INFORMATION

We are a non profit organization that solely acquires our profits from donations and fundraisers Our regular group activities enable the sharing of skills and foster knwoledge.We run cooking classes for families experiencing food poverty to build confidence and help people develop healthier habits around food

VISION

A world where everyone has equitable access to safe, nutritious and culturally appropriate food, supported by sustainable systems that protect the planet, empower communities and promote dignity and health.

MISSION

To bring you the highest quality organic foods

OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
  • To increase community knowledge about balanced nutrition and its impact long-term health by end of the first quarter
  • To advocate for inclusion of healthier food options in local school cafeterias within the next six months
  • To host monthly free cooking workshops focused on preparing healthy meals for low income families
  • To establish a partnership with atleast three local farmers' markets to promote access to fresh produce
